## Overall Impression

SKALA looks unmistakable at first glance and already avoids every eco-SaaS cliché, but its core "eureka" moment is throttled by a clamped `±13.5°` balance, missing methodological assumptions in the UI, and static motion that doesn't feel like real brass/graphite physics. Upgrading the dial/beam to true logarithmic range (`1×–10 000×`), adding a physical `"Wyrównaj wagę (×N)"` counterweight mechanic with damped spring & odometer numeral transitions, and fixing the a11y/touch-target gaps will move this from Good-looking prototype to award-grade instrument.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P0] Balance beam & dial saturate at ~37× and underplay the `1 : N` ratio**
   - **Why it matters:** Breaks the #1 product principle (*"Rząd wielkości ponad wszystko"*): jumping from `1 : 32,6` to `1 : 1 733` produces zero beam/needle motion, and the `17px` ratio inside the dial is smaller than individual pan values.
   - **Fix:** Calibrate the dial across `±110°` with engraved log ticks (`1×`, `10×`, `100×`, `1 000×`), map needle & damped spring physics across `10⁰–10⁴×`, add interpolated odometer numerals, and add an interactive `"Wyrównaj wagę (×N)"` counterweight mode that physically levels the beam to `0°`.
   - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable animate` + `/impeccable bolder`

2. **[P1] `<svg role="img">` hides desktop pan controls from screen readers + missing ARIA states & clipped focus rings**
   - **Why it matters:** Assistive technologies ignore interactive `<select>` and `<a>` inside `<svg role="img">`; missing `aria-live`, `aria-pressed`, and clipped `:focus-visible` inside `.horizon-pills`/`.segmented-control` degrade keyboard and screen-reader usability.
   - **Fix:** Change root balance SVG to `role="group"` (or decorative SVG + HTML overlay), add `aria-live="polite"` ratio/verdict announcer, add `aria-pressed` on all toggle groups, remove `overflow: hidden` focus clipping, respect `prefers-reduced-motion` in JS scrolls, and raise mobile tap targets (`>=36–44px`).
   - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

3. **[P1] Methodological `assumptions` & `ethicalContext` in `evidence.ts` are never displayed + self-comparison bug**
   - **Why it matters:** Users cannot inspect the exact LCA assumptions behind any number without leaving the app, and selecting the same item on both pans outputs broken copy (`"ok. 1× mocniej"`).
   - **Fix:** Render an expandable `"Założenia metodologiczne obu szalek"` panel in the calibration ledger, group `<select>` options with `<optgroup>` by category, unify source pill behavior, and handle `leftId === rightId` cleanly.
   - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

4. **[P2] Decorative "fake metrology" units (`"mm"` on days) & flat single-line vegan chart**
   - **Why it matters:** Printing `"mm"` under a time-horizon bar undermines scientific precision; a single straight diagonal triangle underdelivers the emotional payoff of cumulative vegan impact.
   - **Fix:** Replace `"mm"` with real time ticks (`30d / 1r / 5l / 10l`), add real log decade labels (`0,1 / 1 / 10 / 100 / 1 000`) to `UnifiedScaleSection`, plot all 3 diet baselines on the vegan chart with milestone markers, and fix `.mobile-proportion-seg` to animate via `transform` instead of `flex-grow`.
   - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

5. **[P2] Scroll-hijacking from `UnifiedScaleSection` & lack of shareable duel URL**
   - **Why it matters:** Clicking `"Szalka L"` forces immediate scroll-to-top before the user can also pick `"Szalka P"`, and users cannot share a specific duel link.
   - **Fix:** Replace forced scroll-to-top with a sticky `"Na wadze: [L] vs [P] · Wróć do wagi ↑"` toast/bar and sync `leftId` / `rightId` / `activeMetric` with URL query params (`?l=...&r=...&m=...`).
   - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`
