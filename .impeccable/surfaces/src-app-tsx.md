# Surface Brief: SKALA — Instrument Metrologiczny

## Scope & Mode
- **Primary target:** `src/App.tsx` (single-page interactive web application, desktop + mobile responsive)
- **Visitor mode:** Experience — the precision balance instrument leads from the first viewport; the visitor directly weighs everyday decisions and experiences orders of magnitude.
- **Approved north-star comp:** `.impeccable/mocks/decision/model-pick.png` (chosen by user in direction round; image-gen quota exhausted before generating 2 extra comp variations, so this chosen decision comp serves as the visual contract).

## Direction contract
- **THESIS:** Everyday environmental & ethical decisions belong on one physically honest metrology balance where 10×–10,000× gaps are felt mechanically — refusing eco-green SaaS dashboards, leaf icons, and flattened bar charts.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Warm laboratory enamel off-white (`#ECEAE3`), machined graphite ink & 1.5px hairlines (`#141615`), brushed copper accent (`#A44B27`) for active weight/lens/accumulation, and technical slate blue (`#3B5568`) exclusively for verified scientific source pills. `Archivo` display/UI + `JetBrains Mono` strictly for tabular measurements, vernier scales, and ratios.
- **STORY:** The visitor immediately sees a tilted balance comparing a domestic parcel (`0,38 kg`) against an air-freight China parcel (`12,40 kg`, ratio `1 : 32,6`), swaps in 100 AI prompts or a beef dinner, switches between CO₂e / Woda / Gleba / Energia, explores the cumulative impact of a vegan diet over time, and verifies every number via real LCA study links.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Compact hairline top bar (`SKALA / INSTRUMENT METROLOGICZNY`, 4 unit tabs, `Baza badań LCA` button); full-width interactive tilted balance beam with central vernier ratio dial, two gravity-leveled specimen pans with source badges, millimeter ruler + quick-swap specimen pill rail; split bottom calibration ledger (`KONTEKST ETYCZNY I GREENWASHING` left, `KUMULACJA: DIETA WEGAŃSKA` copper vernier bar right).
- **FORM:** Waga Metrologiczna (Precision Metrology Balance), candidate #1 (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), seed key `65c4df72`.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
